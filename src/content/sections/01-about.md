I’m a high-impact individual contributor and design leader with fifteen years of experience across logistics, healthcare communications, ecommerce, and real-estate software. I’ve designed across B2B, B2C, SaaS, and enterprise products, working with users, teams, and systems at very different scales.

I tend to do my best work when I can go beyond the surface and understand the people, systems, relationships, and constraints shaping their reality.

I’ve been called a UI/UX designer, senior UX designer, UX manager, senior product designer, and design leader. Titles vary from one organization to another, but the work stays the same: understand difficult problems, create shared understanding, and make complex systems easier to use.
