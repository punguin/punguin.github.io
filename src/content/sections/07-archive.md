A few earlier pieces of work that help explain where I started, and what I carried forward.

### Real-estate software

<p class="archive__meta">Lone Wolf Technologies · 2012–2017 · Responsive property search</p>

A responsive property-search redesign with continuously scrolling property imagery, designed in the early period of mainstream responsive web patterns. I wrote HTML, CSS, and jQuery prototypes, challenged the conventional lightbox pattern, and explored continuous image browsing inspired partly by image-heavy web experiences such as Tumblr.

The interface details show their age. The willingness to question the default still feels current.

<p class="archive__meta">Web Designer · Early career</p>
